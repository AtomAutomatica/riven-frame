What was measured

the finding this page illustrates — a filter for judgment, not a proven edge
  • 38 stocks, 1,150 trading days, 12,022 cases (2022 → 2026-09-18): a 5-minute bullish divergence at the day's low ran to a best high of +1.65% by the close. Any regular-hours 5-minute bar: +1.37%. Difference +0.29 points (95% range +0.22 to +0.35, dates as the clusters).
  • The same divergence not at the day's low carries nothing (+1.22% for every 5-minute divergence, under the every-bar baseline). Stacked with a higher timeframe (n 488): +1.56%, no better. Within 1% of the daily 50 (n 2,370): +0.76%, worse.
  • The next-day close carries nothing (+0.46% vs +0.30%, not separable). The money is the same-day exit.
