What was measured

the finding this page illustrates — a filter for judgment, not a proven edge
  • 160 stocks, 1,182 trading days, 54,534 cases (2022 → 2026-09-18): a 5-minute bullish divergence at the day's low ran to a best high of +1.04% by the close. Any regular-hours 5-minute bar: +0.85%. Difference +0.19 points (95% range +0.16 to +0.21, dates as the clusters).
  • The same divergence not at the day's low carries nothing (+0.74% for every 5-minute divergence, under the every-bar baseline). Stacked with a higher timeframe (n 2,135): +1.00%, no better. Within 1% of the daily 50 (n 18,871): +0.48%, worse.
  • The next-day close carries nothing (+0.18% vs +0.14%, not separable). The money is the same-day exit.
