1 · The sequence played forward

The grey line is the median of 3,974 instances of the sequence across every stock: a double bottom with an RSI divergence (the second low sits a median 52 sessions before the retest), a breakout that clears the 20 and the 50 and is 20% off the low (median 28 sessions before), then the first pullback to the rising 50. The blue line is the 22 instances on the bitcoin names alone. Gold is COIN's own path into last Thursday's retest and the day after.

What happens after the retest, in the median case: the path drifts up about +0.3% over 5 sessions and the best high inside those 5 sessions is +3.0%; over 10 sessions the best high is +4.4%. 7% of instances reached +10% within 5 sessions (the COIN class), 13% failed cleanly (down 4% or more with no bounce). The band shows how wide the middle half is: the sequence narrows the names, it does not pick the day.

On the bitcoin names alone (22 instances): 5-session best high +4.4%, 14% reached +10%, 14% failed cleanly. Same shape, wider swings.
